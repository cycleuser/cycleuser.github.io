Capillary Effect - Jurin's Law

h = 2γcos(θ) / (ρgr) γ = surface tension θ = contact angle ρ = liquid density r = tube radius Smaller radius → Higher rise

Communicating Vessels - Pascal's Principle

Connected vessels reach same level. P = ρgh (hydrostatic pressure) At equilibrium: h₁ = h₂ = h₃ = h₄ Regardless of vessel shape!

Sedimentation - Stokes' Law

Terminal velocity: v = 2r²(ρp - ρf)g / (9η) r = particle radius ρp = particle density ρf = fluid density η = viscosity Larger particles settle faster!

Alcohol Precipitation - 醇沉法

Adding ethanol reduces solubility: • Polysaccharides: ~25% (yellow-brown) • Proteins: ~40% (cream white) • Tannins: ~55% (dark brown) • Glycosides: ~70% (green) Higher MW precipitates first!
