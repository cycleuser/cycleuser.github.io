Capillary Effect - Jurin's Law (毛细管效应)

h = 2γcos(θ) / (ρgr)
γ = surface tension (0.0728 N/m for water)
θ = contact angle (0° for water-glass)
ρ = liquid density (1000 kg/m³)
r = tube radius

Communicating Vessels - Pascal's Principle (连通器原理)

Connected vessels reach the same water level regardless of shape.
P = ρgh (hydrostatic pressure)
At equilibrium: h₁ = h₂ = h₃ = ... for same fluid

Sedimentation - Stokes' Law (悬浊液沉淀)

Terminal velocity: v = 2r²(ρp - ρf)g / (9η)
r = particle radius, ρp = particle density
ρf = fluid density, η = dynamic viscosity
Larger/denser particles settle faster

Alcohol Precipitation - 醇沉法

Adding ethanol reduces solubility of compounds.
Different compounds precipitate at different concentrations:
  • Polysaccharides: ~25% (yellow-brown)
  • Proteins: ~40% (cream white)
  • Tannins: ~55% (dark brown)
  • Glycosides: ~70% (green)
Higher molecular weight compounds precipitate first.

Water Purification Process (水净化过程)

Stage 1: Coarse Screen → Removes large debris (>1mm)
Stage 2: Sedimentation Tank → Particles settle by gravity (Stokes' Law)
Stage 3: Sand Filter → Multi-layer filtration removes fine particles (>50μm)
Stage 4: Activated Carbon → Adsorbs dissolved organics via surface chemistry
